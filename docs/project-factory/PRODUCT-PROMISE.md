# Product Promise

## Customer-facing concept

**You describe it. We build it.**

A customer should not need to buy or understand separate coding-agent/model subscriptions. They provide the project brief; Project Factory handles planning, implementation, testing, repair and verification within the package's defined scope.

## Commercial principle

Packages must have explicit scope and resource limits. Never promise unlimited software development at a fixed low price until real delivery telemetry proves the economics.

Example positioning (not final pricing):

- Starter: one defined project, standard scope, basic verification.
- Pro: larger scope, integrations and stronger verification.
- Business: custom scope, deployment/support and higher resource allowance.

The exact prices are a business decision to be validated after measuring model cost, compute, failure/retry rates and human escalation.

## Product moat

The moat is not ownership of a particular model. It is the Project Factory system: task planning, model routing, repository operations, evidence, verification, repair, memory, evaluation and delivery experience.
