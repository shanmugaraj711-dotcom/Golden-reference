# Unit Economics

Track every paid project from the first rupee.

## Formula

`Contribution margin = customer price - variable delivery cost`

## Rule

No paid infrastructure before revenue unless explicitly approved by the owner.

The target is not artificially reported as zero. Record the real cost, including free-tier usage limits and infrastructure consumption, then optimize it.

## Decision rule

After several real projects, use measured margin and quality data to decide whether to automate a bottleneck, change package pricing, or invest revenue into dedicated inference.
